Prioritise effective tax administration

Build taxpayer services and core compliance functions that make the revenue system easier to use and easier to administer.

Policy guidance curated by Dr Emmanuel Munyemana

Strengthen the core functions

Improve taxpayer registration, filing, payment, refunds, dispute resolution and debt collection. Give businesses clear guidance and reliable service standards.

Use risk-based compliance

Combine third-party information and case management to focus audits and enforcement on material risks. Protect taxpayer rights and provide transparent appeal channels.

Sequence digital reforms

Digitise processes that simplify interactions and improve data quality. Pair technology with reliable connectivity, trained staff, clear rules and support for taxpayers.

Policy actions

  • Measure filing, payment, refund and dispute turnaround times.
  • Focus enforcement on high-value compliance risks.
  • Set a taxpayer-service standard and publish results.
