Use debt data for financing decisions

Bring external and domestic debt profiles into fiscal forecasting, borrowing plans and budget risk management.

Policy guidance curated by Dr Emmanuel Munyemana

Build a debt profile

Track stocks, flows, debt service, creditors, currencies and maturities. Pair external debt series with domestic debt bulletins and guarantees.

Link debt to the fiscal plan

Integrate debt service, refinancing and new borrowing into the medium-term fiscal framework and annual budget.

Communicate the position clearly

Publish a regular dashboard covering debt levels, cost, maturity structure and financing plans so decision-makers can act early.

Policy actions

  • Use World Bank IDS for external debt stocks and flows.
  • Integrate IMF fiscal series and national debt bulletins.
  • Connect debt reporting with budget and cash planning.
