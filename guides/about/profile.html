About Us

Dr Emmanuel Munyemana

Data scientist and development policy specialist focused on evidence, public finance and inclusive development.

Policy guidance curated by Dr Emmanuel Munyemana

Research and expertise

Dr Emmanuel Munyemana is a Data scientist with a PhD in Data Science and Econometrics. His research applies econometric methods to household and firm behaviour under resource constraints and uncertainty, including responses to cash transfers, social protection interventions and policy incentives.

Public finance and institutions

With extensive experience working alongside governments and international development organisations, including United Nations agencies such as UNICEF, Dr Munyemana specialises in public finance, social protection, poverty monitoring, national statistical systems and social service delivery.

Africa Fiscal Desk

The desk brings this research and policy experience into a practical resource for fiscal and budget policy across Africa. It connects national sources, institutional data and applied research with clear guidance for decision-makers.
