Use revenue data to set policy priorities

Turn comparable revenue statistics into clear questions for national tax policy, administration and budget planning.

Policy guidance curated by Dr Emmanuel Munyemana

Read the revenue mix

Assess income, consumption, trade, property, resource and non-tax receipts together. Compare the mix with national economic structure and policy goals.

Set measurable reform milestones

Use baseline collections, administration performance and budget forecasts to define targets for compliance, filing, arrears, refunds and tax expenditures.

Report progress in context

Present reference year, country definition and reporting source beside every statistic. Pair revenue trends with service delivery and economic outcomes.

Policy actions

  • Track revenue levels and composition by year.
  • Set institution-level milestones for collection and service improvements.
  • Use revenue dashboards in routine fiscal and budget reviews.
