Policy priorities

Strengthen domestic revenue mobilisation

Build predictable revenue systems that fund public priorities, support productive activity and strengthen trust in public institutions.

Policy guidance curated by Dr Emmanuel Munyemana

Broaden the base and improve compliance

Review exemptions, informality, arrears and compliance gaps. Prioritise fair, predictable rules and service-oriented administration to improve voluntary compliance and sustained collections.

Make tax design work for growth

Protect VAT input credits, simplify filing and refunds, remove avoidable tax distortions to investment and work, and target incentives to additional activity with clear objectives.

Manage revenue performance

Set annual and medium-term targets from the national revenue base. Track tax and non-tax receipts, composition, forecasts and outturns through routine budget reviews.

Policy actions

  • Publish a revenue strategy linked to the medium-term fiscal framework.
  • Set reform milestones for administration, tax expenditures and taxpayer service.
  • Report results by revenue stream and connect them to budget priorities.
