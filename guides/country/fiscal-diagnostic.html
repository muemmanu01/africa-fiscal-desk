Prepare a country fiscal diagnostic

Bring revenue, spending, debt and development outcomes into one practical assessment for policy and budget decisions.

Policy guidance curated by Dr Emmanuel Munyemana

Start with the national fiscal record

Review the approved budget, budget execution reports, fiscal strategy, debt bulletins, audit reports and revenue authority publications.

Build a balanced indicator set

Track tax and non-tax revenue, resource receipts, spending composition and execution, fiscal balance, debt service, public investment and social outcomes.

Prioritise reforms through the budget

Identify the largest fiscal pressures and delivery gaps. Assign each priority an accountable institution, financing path, timetable and result indicator.

Policy actions

  • Build an annual fiscal diagnostic from official national sources.
  • Present trends with a consistent indicator definition and reference year.
  • Use the diagnostic to set policy priorities and monitor budget delivery.
