Connect policy priorities to the budget cycle

Make policy commitments visible in budget preparation, approval, execution, reporting and evaluation.

Policy guidance curated by Dr Emmanuel Munyemana

Prepare a credible medium-term budget

Set revenue and spending baselines, identify fiscal risks and cost priority programmes before ceilings are issued.

Connect allocations to delivery

Use programme budgets, clear performance indicators and procurement plans to align resources with service and investment priorities.

Review execution and close the loop

Monitor commitments, payments and outputs during the year. Publish in-year reports, audit results and evaluation findings to guide the next budget.

Policy actions

  • Align planning, finance and line ministries around shared budget calendars.
  • Match approved allocations with procurement and cash plans.
  • Use in-year evidence and audit results to correct delivery.
