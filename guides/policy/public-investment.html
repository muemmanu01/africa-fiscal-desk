Policy priorities

Raise the return on public investment

Treat public investment as a managed portfolio: select the right projects, deliver them well and protect the assets after completion.

Policy guidance curated by Dr Emmanuel Munyemana

Select projects against national results

Use project appraisal to compare costs, readiness, distributional effects and expected economic and social returns. Publish selection criteria and connect approved projects to funded budget plans.

Control execution and procurement

Coordinate project plans, procurement calendars, cash plans and contract management. Track milestones, commitments, payment and implementation issues across the project cycle.

Protect the value of public assets

Fund maintenance, monitor service levels and evaluate completed projects. Use implementation evidence to improve future appraisal and portfolio choices.

Policy actions

  • Maintain a prioritised, costed and appraised public investment pipeline.
  • Publish project status, procurement milestones and implementation results.
  • Review asset condition and maintenance needs during each budget cycle.
