Connect fiscal policy with social protection outcomes

Use evidence on household needs and programme performance to align public finance with inclusive growth and effective service delivery.

Policy guidance curated by Dr Emmanuel Munyemana

Link budgets to household outcomes

Track programme coverage, adequacy, timeliness and access alongside budget allocations and execution.

Use research to improve design

Assess how households respond to transfers and social protection. Use evidence to refine eligibility, payment delivery and links with employment and essential services.

Protect delivery through fiscal planning

Cost commitments over the medium term, coordinate national and local delivery and track whether funds reach intended beneficiaries.

Policy actions

  • Report coverage and payment timeliness with expenditure execution.
  • Use programme evidence in medium-term budget and financing decisions.
  • Coordinate social protection with employment, health and education services.
