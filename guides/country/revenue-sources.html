Country tools

Find and use national revenue sources

Start policy analysis with the institutions that administer, collect and report national revenue.

Policy guidance curated by Dr Emmanuel Munyemana

Use primary legal sources

Consult current tax legislation, Finance Acts, regulations and revenue authority guidance to establish tax obligations, exemptions and administration rules.

Review official revenue performance

Use annual revenue reports, budget estimates, in-year collections, audit reports and tax expenditure statements to diagnose performance.

Connect the national view to comparators

Use OECD, ATAF and IMF data to compare trends and composition, then return to national sources to design country-owned reforms.

Policy actions

  • Bookmark the finance ministry, tax authority, treasury and statistical office.
  • Record the report year and revenue definition used in each analysis.
  • Link every policy recommendation to the law, dataset or official report that supports it.
