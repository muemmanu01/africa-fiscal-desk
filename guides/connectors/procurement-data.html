Data connectors

Make procurement data part of fiscal delivery

Connect project, procurement and budget information to strengthen public investment planning and delivery.

Policy guidance curated by Dr Emmanuel Munyemana

Publish a clear pipeline

Present project purpose, sponsor, location, financing, procurement stage and expected delivery milestones in one searchable register.

Connect commitments to budgets

Link procurement plans and contract awards to approved projects, annual allocations and budget execution.

Use open information to improve delivery

Make notice dates, eligibility requirements, deadlines and award results easy to find. Use participation and implementation data to improve future procurement.

Policy actions

  • Maintain one institution-wide procurement and project calendar.
  • Link project data to budget codes and public investment plans.
  • Report awards, implementation progress and completion results.
