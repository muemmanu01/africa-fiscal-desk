About Us

Dr Emmanuel Munyemana is a Data scientist with a PhD in Data Science and Econometrics. He is a renowned development policy specialist whose research applies econometric methods to understand household and firm behaviour under resource constraints and uncertainty. His work explores how low-income households respond to cash transfers and social protection interventions, and how small and medium-sized enterprises adapt to policy incentives and economic uncertainty.

With extensive experience working alongside governments and international development organisations, including United Nations agencies such as UNICEF, Dr Munyemana specialises in public finance, social protection, poverty monitoring, national statistical systems and social service delivery. He is particularly interested in translating rigorous research and data-driven insights into stronger public institutions, more effective policies and inclusive development outcomes.

Decision framework

From fiscal diagnosis to delivery

1. Diagnose the fiscal position

Bring revenue composition, resource and non-tax receipts, tax expenditures, fiscal balances, debt service and budget execution into one medium-term fiscal picture.

2. Set country-owned priorities

Use the national budget, revenue laws, medium-term framework and official data to identify the reforms with the largest contribution to fiscal space, growth and service delivery.

3. Sequence reforms for delivery

Strengthen core tax administration, simplify rules, improve voluntary compliance and protect timely refunds. Then expand analytics and targeted incentives where institutions can deliver and evaluate results.

4. Measure results and reinvest gains

Track revenue collection, spending execution, debt service and service outcomes. Use findings to improve programme design, strengthen accountability and direct resources toward inclusive development.
