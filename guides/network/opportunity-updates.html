Connect Africa Fiscal Desk

Follow public investment and procurement opportunities

Use official issuer notices and project data to find opportunities connected with African development programmes.

Policy guidance curated by Dr Emmanuel Munyemana

Track official notices

Follow African Development Bank and World Bank project and procurement sources for tenders, expressions of interest, project documents and contract awards.

Record the information that matters

Organise each notice by issuer, country, sector, status, eligibility, deadline and direct source link. Keep the underlying notice attached to every update.

Connect opportunity information to policy

Use project and procurement data to understand implementation pipelines, local participation and delivery progress. Share verified notices with relevant partners.

Policy actions

  • Check official procurement sources regularly.
  • Record issuer, status, eligibility and deadline for every notice.
  • Share direct source links and update dates with the network.
