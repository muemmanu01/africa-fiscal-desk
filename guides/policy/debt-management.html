Policy priorities

Integrate debt strategy with the budget

Manage borrowing, refinancing and fiscal risks as part of the same plan that funds public services and investment.

Policy guidance curated by Dr Emmanuel Munyemana

Map the debt portfolio

Maintain a current view of domestic and external debt stocks, creditors, currencies, interest costs, maturities, guarantees and contingent liabilities.

Plan borrowing and cash needs together

Coordinate debt issuance with budget financing requirements, cash forecasts and project readiness. Use maturity and currency profiles to plan refinancing and manage exposure.

Strengthen governance and reporting

Publish the debt strategy, annual borrowing plan and regular debt bulletins. Set clear approval and reporting responsibilities across treasury, debt office and spending agencies.

Policy actions

  • Use a medium-term debt strategy and annual borrowing plan.
  • Integrate debt service and refinancing needs in fiscal forecasts and budget ceilings.
  • Report guarantees, arrears and other fiscal risks alongside public debt.
