About Us

A practical approach to fiscal and budget policy

Translate rigorous research and official data into stronger institutions, effective policy and inclusive development outcomes.

Policy guidance curated by Dr Emmanuel Munyemana

Start from the decision

Define the policy question, the institution responsible and the decision timeline. Assemble country data and primary sources that inform that decision.

Apply evidence to country conditions

Combine administrative data, fiscal trends, programme evidence and applied research. Identify how households, firms and institutions respond to policy changes.

Measure delivery and improve policy

Set clear outcomes, monitor implementation and publish results. Use what works to strengthen public institutions, budget choices and service delivery.

Policy actions

  • Use source-linked analysis and evidence-based recommendations.
  • Track household, firm and institutional responses to policy.
  • Feed implementation results into the next budget and reform cycle.
